# Footer Status Bar Redesign

The shared footer is now a compact Industrial Blue command-status bar rather than an undifferentiated line of metadata. The left brand block presents **Universe Civilization: Empire at War**, the developer, and the linked publisher. The right status deck separates Version, Patch, Update, Build, and Time into individually legible modules; Patch remains interactive and opens patch notes.

The live Stargate Command page was checked at the bottom of the shell after production deployment. The desktop footer displayed the brand block and all five status modules in one concise row, with readable Industrial Blue borders and status contrast. Production health remained 100 after the deployment.
