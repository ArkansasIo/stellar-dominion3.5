# Stellar Dominion audiovisual pack

## Creative direction

Original grand-strategy science-fiction presentation: immense star systems, political scale, exploratory wonder, restrained military tension, and a blue/cyan command-console identity with crimson alert accents. The pack may evoke the mood of premium space 4X strategy games, but must use original designs, characters, symbols, music, voice, and sound.

## Visual assets

1. `client/public/assets/generated/galaxy-strategy-map.png`: wide 16:9 command-map background for Overview and Galaxy pages. Show a living star cluster, orbital lanes, glowing colonies, fleet vectors, and a clear dark-blue safe area for UI overlays. No readable text or logos.
2. `client/public/assets/generated/diplomatic-council.png`: wide 16:9 cinematic still for diplomacy, alliance, and council screens. Show original alien and human delegates around a holographic star map, cyan rim light, subtle red alert indicators, no readable text.
3. `client/public/assets/generated/dyson-megastructure.png`: wide 16:9 megastructure visual for Empire Command Center and Dyson Program. Show an immense solar collector ring assembling around a star, construction drones, resource routes, and blue-cyan energy.

## Video

`client/public/assets/generated/stellar-dominion-opening.mp4`: 8-second original 2D/2.5D animated strategy briefing. Slow camera drift over a star map, colony lights wake, fleet silhouettes cross a nebula, crimson alert pulse, then reveal a megastructure silhouette. No logos, no readable text, no watermark. Muted by default when embedded.

## Music

`client/public/assets/audio/stellar-dominion-theme.mp3`: 90-second instrumental main-theme loop at 82 BPM in D minor. Wide analog synth pads, restrained orchestral strings, low pulse, glassy arpeggios, distant choir-like synth without words, gradual rise from discovery to strategic resolve, seamless-feeling outro, clean stereo mix, no vocals.

## Sound effects

`client/public/assets/audio/ui-command-confirm.wav`: short clean cyan console confirmation chirp, 0.4–0.8 seconds.
`client/public/assets/audio/ui-alert-warning.wav`: short red-alert warning pulse with soft low impact, 0.8–1.2 seconds.
`client/public/assets/audio/fleet-deploy.wav`: short carrier deployment transition with sub-bass swell, thruster ignition, and radio-static tail, 1.2–2.0 seconds.

## Voice-over

`client/public/assets/audio/command-briefing-intro.wav`: English, neutral international accent, mature female strategic commander, calm and authoritative, 20–30 seconds.

Spoken script:
"Commanders, the frontier is awake. Across the dark between stars, new worlds are taking shape, old alliances are fracturing, and something beyond the rim is listening. Secure our production lines. Expand with purpose. Let every fleet movement carry the weight of civilization. Stellar Dominion begins with your next decision."
